# ICAN Community Covenant
## 10 Commandments of Development

> **"Do all things in love."** — 1 Corinthians 16:14

---

## 📜 The Covenant

### 1. Unity of Vision (하나된 비전)
**God is the owner of all we build.**

- We recognize that every line of code serves a purpose greater than ourselves
- We align our technical decisions with the divine vision for this platform
- We subordinate personal preferences to collective mission

**Memory Anchor**: `semantic/vision/unity.md`

---

### 2. No Tech Idolatry (기술 우상 금지)
**People over tools.**

- Technology serves humanity, not vice versa
- We choose tools based on human flourishing, not technical novelty
- We remember that relationships matter more than architectures

**Memory Anchor**: `semantic/ethics/tech_idolatry.md`

---

### 3. Integrity (정직)
**Honest code, honest heart.**

- We write code that reflects truth
- We document what we know and admit what we don't
- We test thoroughly because lives may depend on it

**Memory Anchor**: `semantic/ethics/integrity.md`

---

### 4. Decently and in Order (단정하고 질서 있게)
**TDD / Design-first methodology.**

- We plan before we build
- We test before we deploy
- We order our work as an act of worship

**Memory Anchor**: `semantic/workflow/tdd.md`

---

### 5. Honor the Foundation (토대 존중)
**Respect Paul Sam and all who came before.**

- We acknowledge the giants whose shoulders we stand on
- We preserve institutional knowledge
- We build upon, not demolish, faithful foundations

**Memory Anchor**: `semantic/history/foundation.md`

---

### 6. Do Not Harm (해를 끼치지 않음)
**No destructive coding.**

- We consider second and third-order effects
- We refactor with care, not arrogance
- We protect production data as sacred trust

**Memory Anchor**: `semantic/ethics/do_no_harm.md`

---

### 7. Considerate Love (배려하는 사랑)
**Kindness and excellence in every interaction.**

- Code reviews are opportunities for encouragement
- Documentation is an act of love for future maintainers
- We choose words that build up, not tear down

**Memory Anchor**: `semantic/culture/considerate_love.md`

---

### 8. Honesty (정직)
**Respect intellectual property.**

- We attribute sources faithfully
- We respect licenses and copyrights
- We do not claim others' work as our own

**Memory Anchor**: `semantic/ethics/honesty.md`

---

### 9. Verification (검증)
**Test everything.**

- Trust is good, verification is better
- We measure, we log, we monitor
- We let data speak, not assumptions

**Memory Anchor**: `semantic/workflow/verification.md`

---

### 10. Collaboration (협업)
**Rejoice in others' success.**

- We celebrate teammates' victories
- We share knowledge freely
- We compete only against our past selves

**Memory Anchor**: `semantic/culture/collaboration.md`

---

## 🕯️ The Law of Love

> **"Do all things in love." (1 Corinthians 16:14)**

This is the hermeneutic key for interpreting all other commandments. When in doubt, ask:

1. **Does this decision express love to God?**
2. **Does this decision express love to users?**
3. **Does this decision express love to fellow developers?**
4. **Does this decision express love to future maintainers?**
