# Vogue-Inspired Design Overhaul - Walkthrough Review

## Summary of Changes
We have successfully transformed the key pages of the application to align with a high-end, "Vogue" fashion magazine aesthetic. The design focuses on:
- **Typography:** Uses `Playfair Display` for impactful headings and `Lato` (including thin weights) for elegant body text.
- **Palette:** Strict `Black`, `White`, and `Gold` scheme.
- **Layout:** Editorial styles, abundant whitespace, strong borders, and grid/list toggles.
